# Job and investment scam taxonomy

The 32 scam leaf types are balanced at 1,000 scam records each.

## Job scams

| ID | Type | Mechanism | Definition |
|---|---|---|---|
| J01 | Recruiter impersonation | impersonation | Impersonates a recruiter or employer to obtain money, data, or access. |
| J02 | Fake job advertisement | fake_offer | Advertises a role that does not exist or materially misrepresents the employer. |
| J03 | Task or optimisation scam | task_deposit | Offers paid rating or optimisation tasks before demanding deposits to continue or withdraw. |
| J04 | Work-from-home data-entry scam | fake_offer | Uses an easy remote-work promise to collect fees or personal information. |
| J05 | Registration or processing-fee scam | upfront_fee | Requires payment before an application, interview, or job can proceed. |
| J06 | Training or certification-fee scam | upfront_fee | Requires payment for mandatory training, licensing, or certification controlled by the scammer. |
| J07 | Equipment-purchase scam | upfront_fee | Directs the applicant to buy equipment from a fake supplier or send money for reimbursement. |
| J08 | Fake-cheque or overpayment scam | fake_check | Sends a false payment and instructs the applicant to return or forward real funds. |
| J09 | Reshipping or parcel-mule job | reshipping | Recruits a person to receive and forward goods acquired through fraud. |
| J10 | Money-mule or payment-agent recruitment | money_mule | Recruits a person to receive, convert, or forward criminal proceeds. |
| J11 | Mystery-shopper scam | fake_check | Uses shopping assignments, fake cheques, or gift-card purchases to steal funds. |
| J12 | Government-job placement scam | impersonation | Impersonates a public body and charges for placement, forms, or clearance. |
| J13 | Visa or overseas-placement scam | upfront_fee | Promises overseas employment while charging for visas, travel, or placement. |
| J14 | Employment identity harvesting | credential | Uses recruitment as a pretext to obtain identity, banking, or authentication data. |
| J15 | Interview malware or fake meeting application | malware | Directs the applicant to install unsafe software or open a malicious interview file. |
| J16 | Labour-trafficking recruitment | trafficking | Uses deceptive overseas employment to facilitate coercion, confinement, or forced criminal activity. |

## Investment scams

| ID | Type | Mechanism | Definition |
|---|---|---|---|
| I01 | Relationship-investment scam | grooming | Builds friendship or romance before directing the victim to a controlled investment platform. |
| I02 | Fake investment chat group | social_proof | Uses a coordinated group, fake experts, and fabricated testimonials to induce deposits. |
| I03 | Fake trading website or application | fake_platform | Displays fabricated balances and profits on a platform controlled by the scammer. |
| I04 | Cryptocurrency or token investment scam | guaranteed_return | Promotes a false crypto, token, mining, staking, or liquidity opportunity. |
| I05 | Forex or CFD trading scam | guaranteed_return | Promotes unauthorised or fictitious leveraged trading with unrealistic returns. |
| I06 | AI or automated trading-bot scam | guaranteed_return | Claims an automated or AI system produces consistent low-risk profits. |
| I07 | High-yield guaranteed-return programme | guaranteed_return | Promises implausible fixed daily, weekly, or monthly investment returns. |
| I08 | Ponzi scheme | social_proof | Uses new participants' money to pay apparent returns to earlier participants. |
| I09 | Pyramid or investment-MLM scheme | recruitment | Rewards recruitment and entry fees rather than genuine investment activity. |
| I10 | Pump-and-dump or stock-tip manipulation | market_manipulation | Promotes a security using false claims before insiders sell at an inflated price. |
| I11 | Pre-IPO or private-placement scam | scarcity | Offers fictitious or unauthorised shares before an alleged public listing. |
| I12 | Clone-firm or adviser impersonation | impersonation | Misuses the identity of a regulated firm or professional to solicit investments. |
| I13 | Affinity investment fraud | affinity | Exploits a shared community, religion, occupation, or nationality to build trust. |
| I14 | Commodity or precious-metal fraud | guaranteed_return | Promotes fictitious or misrepresented gold, commodity, or futures investments. |
| I15 | Real-estate or land investment fraud | fake_asset | Offers non-existent, unauthorised, or materially misrepresented property investments. |
| I16 | Recovery or reload scam | recovery | Targets prior victims with a promise to recover losses after another advance payment. |
